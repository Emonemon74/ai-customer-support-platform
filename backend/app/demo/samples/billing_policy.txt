Acme Cloud — Billing & Refund Policy

---PAGE---
1. Subscription plans

Acme Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Team, and Enterprise. The Starter plan
is billed at 12 USD per user per month. The Team plan is billed at 29 USD per
user per month and includes priority email support. Enterprise pricing is
custom and negotiated with the sales team.

All plans are billed monthly in advance. Annual billing is available and
provides a 20 percent discount compared to monthly billing.

---PAGE---
2. Refunds

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of their first payment.
Refund requests made after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but the
subscription can still be cancelled to stop future charges.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business
days. Acme Cloud does not offer partial refunds for unused time on monthly
plans.

For annual plans cancelled after the 30 day window, a prorated credit is
applied to the account but no cash refund is issued.

---PAGE---
3. Failed payments and dunning

If a scheduled payment fails, Acme Cloud retries the charge after 3 days, then
again after 7 days. If all three attempts fail, the account is downgraded to
read-only mode. Data is retained for 60 days after downgrade before deletion.

To restore full access, update the payment method in Settings > Billing and the
outstanding invoice will be charged immediately.

---PAGE---
4. Taxes and invoices

Invoices are available in Settings > Billing > Invoice history. VAT is added
for customers in the European Union unless a valid VAT identification number is
on file. Invoices cannot be edited after they are issued; a corrective credit
note is issued instead.
