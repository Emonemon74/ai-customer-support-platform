Acme Cloud — Team Onboarding Manual

---PAGE---
1. Inviting your team

Workspace owners and admins can invite members from Settings > Members. Invites
are sent by email and expire after 14 days. A pending invite does not consume a
paid seat until it is accepted.

There are four roles: Owner, Admin, Member, and Viewer. Only Owners can delete
the workspace or change the billing contact.

---PAGE---
2. Importing existing content

You can bulk import documents from Google Drive, Dropbox, or a zip file of
PDFs. Imports run in the background and you receive an email when they finish.
The maximum size for a single imported file is 20 MB. Files that fail to parse
are listed in the import report with the reason.

---PAGE---
3. Setting up your first support workspace

Create a conversation for each customer or topic, then upload the relevant
policy documents into that conversation. Ask questions in natural language and
the assistant answers using only the documents in that conversation, with
citations to the source page.

We recommend keeping fewer than 50 documents per conversation for the best
answer quality.

---PAGE---
4. Keyboard shortcuts

Press C to create a new conversation, U to upload a document, and the forward
slash key to focus the message box. Press G then S to jump to Settings.
